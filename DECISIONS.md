# DECISIONS.md — choices made where the spec was silent (or deliberately deviated)

Format: date · milestone · decision · reason.

## Iteration 1 — M0–M3 (scaffold, map & camera, the Operative, LOS & fog)

- **Display scaling.** Integer scaling is done in *device* pixels (backing store = logical resolution, CSS size = W×scale/dpr). The logical size flexes between 400–560 × 240–300 instead of a fixed 480×270; the integer scale is chosen so the logical height is closest to 270. Reason: on phones a fixed 480×270 either letterboxes heavily or needs non-integer scaling (e.g. iPhone Safari landscape 844×340 CSS @3× → 560×255 at scale 4). The HUD is laid out relative to edges so it adapts.
- **HUD button size** is computed from the CSS-px-per-logical-px ratio so every button is ≥ 44 CSS px (26–44 logical px), with 4 px of extra invisible touch slop.
- **Tap timing.** Presses up to the long-press threshold (450 ms) with < 8 px movement count as taps (spec: 250 ms). Presses between 250 and 450 ms were otherwise dead input, which felt unresponsive on touch. The walk order is still dispatched on release immediately; a second tap within 300 ms upgrades to Run.
- **Sprites.** Infantry upper bodies are authored as palette-indexed string grids per view (`src/render/spriteData/humanoid.js`); legs, arms, weapons and poses (walk/run/crouch/prone/fire/death) are composed procedurally per frame and auto-outlined. Flora, terrain textures and props are procedural painters writing palette colours into pixel buffers. All sprite keys can be overridden by PNGs via `MANIFEST` in `sprites.js`.
- **Terrain rendering** is baked per 16×16-tile chunk (256×256 px) at load: organic jagged transitions between terrain types (priority-based), shorelines with foam, cliff faces (cellular "stacked boulder" shading), ramps, drop shadows, low props (sandbags, crates, rocks, rubble, walls, fences, bridges, wrecks) and ground decoration (flowers, pebbles, tufts, bones, logs). Trees and structures are y-sorted sprites.
- **Cliffs.** A tile is an impassable cliff when its south or east neighbour is lower (unless that neighbour is a ramp leading up into it). North/west plateau edges are walkable but only connect to lower ground through ramps. Ramp tiles sit on the lower level and point at the higher neighbour.
- **Elevation brightness** is +9 % per level instead of +6 %; at 6 % plateaus were indistinguishable from the valley on phone screens.
- **LOS adjacency.** Any tile within 1.5 tiles is always visible (covers the spec's "adjacent higher tile peek", including diagonals whose supercover line touches the plateau corner).
- **Building blocker height** defaults to 2.5 (spec says "2.0, per building definition"). With 2.0 the §7.3 formula (`elev + blockH > max(eO, eT)`) lets a level-2 observer see over buildings, contradicting the spec's own summary ("buildings still block unless you are on level 3"). 2.5 satisfies both.
- **Movement smoothing.** A* runs on the 8-connected tile grid; the resulting path is string-pulled where a straight line stays on tiles of equal or lower cost and legal elevation steps, so the Operative walks at any angle instead of zig-zagging.
- **Operative readability.** A double selection ring, a bobbing locator chevron above WREN and an x-ray silhouette when occluded by trees keep the 12-px soldier findable on a big map.
- **Scenes folder.** Game/menus live in `src/scenes/` (gameScene, menus, settings); the HUD and minimap are in `src/ui/` as specified.
- **Map generation.** `tools/mapgen/mN.js` builds each mission (layers + entities + objectives) from the spec schematic with domain-warped edges, polylines for roads/rivers and explicit ramps; `node tools/mapgen/build.js` writes `src/missions/data/mN.js`. Mission modules (`src/missions/mN.js`) add custom hooks.
- **Extra terrain/overlay codes** beyond §19.3: terrain `i` ice, `a` ash, `p` gravel; overlay `q` small rocks (cover), `u` rubble (walkable cover), `y` wreck (cover), `j` bush (concealment 0.6), `z` spore crystal cluster (cover), `P` pine (dense forest variant).

## Iteration 2 — M4–M5 (enemies & stealth, combat & scope)

- **Critic-1 fixes**: lock-step camera follow (camera = rounded target + decaying integer offset; updated in render with the same interpolation alpha, so WREN never wobbles); safe-area insets (with a 32 CSS px fallback on ≥2:1 touch screens); objectives panel collapsed to one line by default, fit-to-content, auto-peeks on start/changes; smarter tap targets (tapping water/cliff picks the nearby reachable tile cheapest to reach); ramp landings are never cliffs; long-press info stays 2 s and sits away from the finger; menus sized from the 44-CSS-px button size (title 2×2, settings two columns); new glyphs (▾ ▸ &); prone pose wider; cover pose leans + shield badge; plateau tops +warm tint; tapping a destination no longer snaps the camera back (it re-follows when WREN is about to leave the view).
- **Kill witnesses (the spec references a missing "§9.5")**: when a unit dies, members of its alertGroup within the weapon's noise radius, and anyone who saw it fall, switch to ALERTED and search expanding rings around the body; the group goes to Caution. The rifle's noise event excludes that group so the two rules don't stack.
- **Vision cones** of visible enemies within 16 tiles are always drawn (LOS-clipped, tinted by state) — the spec's tutorial teaches "reading vision cones", so the player needs to see them outside debug.
- **Enemy gunfire is noise** (8 tiles): comrades who hear it search where the shooter was aiming.
- **Awareness eye** shows Suspicious as soon as any meter passes 0.1 (early warning), Detected when any enemy has WREN at 1.0 and in sight.
- **Enemy reaction time** 0.35–0.75 s before the first burst after detection.
- **Scope aiming model**: the reticle stays centred; dragging pans the aim 1:1 in scope px (mouse movement on desktop is relative too; click, Space or Enter fires; WASD nudges; Shift holds breath; right-click/Esc exits). Pan is limited to ±2 tiles around the (moving) target.
- **Breath**: releasing early refills the breath meter (0.8 s/s) without a gasp; the 2 s gasp + 4 s cooldown happen only when the 3 s hold is exhausted.
- **Scope feedback**: dead targets topple (rotating close-up) instead of switching sprites; misses near a target spook it (alert, no damage).
- **Scope sprites** (40×64, front/back/right; left mirrored) are procedural painters with hit zones defined in the same file; zone priorities: head 5, gear 4 (belt/tank/pod/radio), torso/body 3, limbs 1.
- **Critic-2 fixes (round 1)**: the round is sampled at the reticle *before* the recoil kick (regression test `tests/scope.test.js`); enemies that lose WREN in combat switch to ALERTED and search the LKP rings, then return (`tests/ai.test.js`); corpses clear their hit flash; vision cones use a screen-aligned ordered-dither fill (overlaps never stack) with a dotted rim; the scope eases to a new target and only re-targets units in range and LOS; Run & Gun approaches to ~2.5 tiles and stops when the target dies; enemy tap radius 18 px; head/torso zones overlap (no neck gap); fine 1-px blood spray at the impact point inside the scope; stagger shudder and white hit flash on scope close-ups; lungs icon for HOLD BREATH; tracers 2 px.
- **Base footprints**: alert groups may name an `area` (their base). The "Detected for 3 s continuously inside the base" auto-Alarm only fires while WREN is inside that area; roaming patrols/lone sentries (no area) never auto-escalate. "Reinforcements incoming" is only announced when the group has a barracks. Witnesses from another group put their own group on Caution too.
- **Mission 1 ford**: a scrubby west-bank spit and a dry east shore make the tutorial's first shot possible from land (7 tiles across the ford).

## Iteration 3 — M6–M7 (explosives, vehicles, structures, C4; missions framework + Mission 1)

- **Critic-2 carry-overs**: "They lost you" + LKP only when a surviving spotter lost sight (killing the lone spotter says "Contact down."); peripheral glances don't pick out a hunkered target in concealment, and sight-based suspicion investigates a fuzzy point 1.5–2.5 tiles off WREN and stops ~2 tiles short; the officer's radio call gets a dark plate with a 3 s countdown bar plus an OVERWATCH shout; the scope topple runs on real time; bigger impact spray with a dark edge; cone fill only for cones near WREN or alert units (rims otherwise, with a dark shadow pixel); the engage planner penalises tiles inside the target's own cone; every Alarm is announced and shown with hazard-striped bars; scope buttons are opaque, the ticker hides while scoped and stamps sit at the top of the scope.
- **Structures** are procedural 3/4-view sprites (roof = footprint shifted up by the building height, plus a south face) with cast shadows. Snipeable parts are zones in sprite pixels: tower/MG gunner, searchlight, coolant cell, comms dish, siren, fuel-depot barrels. Structures keep their last-known look in fog.
- **Gunners & turrets** are static "emplacement" units: they perceive like infantry (towers from one level higher, sweeping ±60° over 6 s), fire MG bursts or cannon shells, and a dead post is re-manned by an alerted infantry unit after 10 s beside it. Turrets need power; a destroyed/cut power plant blinds turrets and jammers.
- **Comms**: a destroyed comms array (or a sniped dish, 60 s) makes that group's Alarm local (12 tiles) with no reinforcements.
- **Reinforcements**: during Alarm the group's barracks sends 2 troops every 20 s up to `reinforceCap`; a vehicle bay with `spawnOnAlarm` sends one vehicle per Alarm.
- **Vehicles** use sprite stacking (layered colour functions rotated to 8 facings). Driver (front/side) or view slit (front only, 3×2/4×2 px) disables them permanently; passengers dismount after 2 s. Jerrycan/fuel tank shots detonate them. Vehicles turn before driving off and prefer roads. Tall-grass flattening by vehicles is not implemented (cosmetic).
- **Explosion chains** run on world time (respect slow-mo & pause). Barrels/gas are props (converted from overlay `x`).
- **C4**: tapping a C4-able target proposes it (amber marker + PLANT button; tapping again also confirms); the C4 button enters a "tap a target" mode. The charge counts against the loadout only once planted.
- **OBSERVE** needs LOS to the area centre *or one of its four neighbours* (a single post in the middle of an outpost shouldn't make it unobservable from one side).
- **Missions**: triggers/actions per §19.3 in a runner; tutorial prompts are dismissible panels shown once per save; the dropship refuses to land while alert/visible enemies are within 10 tiles of the LZ (circles) unless smoke is popped. Briefing (typewriter + fog-covered preview + objectives + intel + loadout) and debrief (stars, medals, stats) screens; progress saved.
- **Mission flow until the campaign map (M10)**: Title → Briefing → Mission → Debrief → next available mission.
- **Critic-3 fixes (round 1)**: M1's goat trail now reaches the knoll (south ramp, bush cover), ford sentry is `important`, WREN starts with 1 medkit + 1 smoke and M1 enemies deal ×0.75 damage (`enemyDamageMult`); the map validator requires LOS to each OBSERVE area within 8 tiles; only player-caused deaths count as kills (NOT friendly fire and chain blasts they set off don't); Lobbers won't throw next to allies; the disabled-vehicle vision cut is its own field (the FSM no longer resets it); fuel haulers and Skitters explode when they die and chain; longer explosions with a smoke column; the LZ is marked (blinking dashed box + green smoke, drawn over fog) with screen-edge arrows to the LZ and the dropship; the dropship flies in over the map, circles inside the map, lands, waits for WREN, who walks aboard, then lifts off before the debrief; debrief buttons are centred and ignore input for 1.1 s; tutorial prompts queue (never replace each other), count as seen only after 3 s on screen, use the 5×7 font and sit left of the minimap; the "First shot" tip waits until the ford sentry is actually visible and "Bodies" only fires for bodies WREN left; hunting vehicles stop 3.5 tiles short, wait for vehicles/WREN in front (re-path after 4 s) and use turning hysteresis; the scope draws fog under the sprites; turrets/towers have outlined silhouettes and rubble is scorched; Pause/Centre are full-size buttons; tapping a building with snipeable parts when C4 can't be used opens the scope (plus a one-time tip about long-press); debug runs (`?god ?at ?reveal ?debug ?map`) never write progress or tutorial flags.
- `?map=test` has a small NOT outpost (power plant, turret, barracks, fuel depot, tower, MG nest, comms array, vehicles) for exercising these systems.

## Iteration 4 — M8 (Missions 2–4: snow tracks & blizzard, convoy escort, boss Vrask)

- **Critic-3 carry-overs**: hunting vehicles also stop 3.5 tiles from WREN himself (not just from their fuzzy search goal); an open vehicle whose driver is shot has nobody on the gun (silenced, blind); tutorial tips fold into a one-line "TIP · TITLE ▸" banner after 6 s, or after 2.5 s once an enemy is on to WREN (tap the banner to re-open); "Reinforcements incoming" only when the barracks stands, comms are up and the cap isn't reached; the dropship's orbit blends in from where it is; extraction radio lines replace stale queued lines; the LZ has a bright rising green smoke column drawn over fog; the scope can lift to the top of tall structures (aim limit follows the highest-priority part); x-ray silhouette of WREN behind buildings; a progress bar over WREN for timed actions (C4, medkit); the briefing map labels objectives A, B, C… and LZ; only the 4 nearest cones that could reach WREN are filled (an Alarm no longer paints the screen red).
- **GOD friendlies** (pilot, scientist: palette-swapped Operative bodies without weapons; medical trucks) are targets for the NOT with the same perception rules: each enemy keeps a second meter for the friendly it sees best, WREN has priority whenever he is visible. Units flagged `targetsTrucks` (M4 ambushers) only engage trucks within 7 tiles. Persons are mission-critical only when the data says `critical: true` (M3/M4 pilots are optional).
- **Rescue**: tap a seen captive → WREN walks next to them; standing still beside them for 2 s frees them (bar + "FREE"/"HELP UP" label). Freed persons follow (tap to toggle FOLLOW/HOLD; group button with 2+).
- **Convoy (M4)**: ADVANCE/HOLD; trucks drive checkpoint to checkpoint on the lead's breadcrumb trail (3-tile spacing), close up behind the lead when it stops, auto-stop when a visible enemy is within 6 tiles. The ESCORT objective shows "(n/2)"; SURVIVE shows a countdown. M4's ambush and checkpoint 1 moved east so the convoy's start is out of sight, plus a start ramp to the north rim.
- **NOT convoy (M3)**: riders (`mounted`) are hidden inside their vehicle and skipped by AI, perception, scope and rendering. At stops they get out once their vehicle has pulled up; Vrask walks the inspection loop and everyone walks back (scripted) before the column moves on. On Alarm the column races to the summit bunker; if Vrask is on foot far from his Crawler he runs there himself (scripted, doesn't stop to fight) and is untouchable for 2 minutes. A cut road (blown bridge) halts the column and Vrask gets out to look. M3 got a two-step ramp so vehicles can actually reach the summit.
- **Vehicle deadlock**: two vehicles nose to nose would wait forever; after ~2 s one squeezes past (brief overlap) — preferable to a jammed column.
- **Bridges**: C4 on a demolishable tile drops every connected bridge tile into deep water (units on it die).
- **Terrain**: tileable textures are sampled at different offsets across large dithered noise regions so the 64-px period doesn't show as a grid; a desert "shallow" (dry riverbed) gets damp banks instead of white foam.
- Missions 2–4 are available from the campaign flow (Title → Briefing → Mission → Debrief → next).

## Iteration 5 — M9 (strategic strike, Missions 5–7: night, searchlights, tunnels, shield, checkpoints)

- **Where the archive stopped**: the M9 systems (`strike/strike.js`, `world/tunnels.js`, `render/lighting.js`, `missions/checkpoint.js`) and Missions 5–7 (maps, scripts) existed with unit tests, but nothing in the game scene created them — the STRIKE button said "NOT YET AVAILABLE", nights weren't dark, `saveCheckpoint` was never defined and M5–M7 weren't in `app.available`. This iteration wires them in.
- **Dijkstra fields settle each tile once.** `Pathfinder.field()` had no closed set and stores distances as float32; when a distance rounded *up* on storage, duplicate heap pops kept re-relaxing their neighbours and the duplicates cascaded. On Mission 5's cost mix even a 60-cost field never finished (the validator took ~4 min for M5; in game, tapping an enemy, a C4 target or water would have frozen the page). Fixed, with a regression test comparing the field to A* on M5; all seven maps now validate in ~0.5 s.
- **Damage over time is banked, not rounded.** `damageOperative` rounds every hit up to ≥ 1 HP; fire and fallout arrive as per-tick slivers, so 4 dps fallout was really 30 dps (and 10 dps fire likewise). Fire/fallout damage now accumulates and applies whole points.
- **Hardened structures** take 25 % once: the strike used to pre-multiply by 0.25 *and* `structures.damage()` multiplied again (≈ 6 %). Two strikes on the shielded Spire now do the spec's 25 % + 25 %.
- **Designator input**: STRIKE (or N) enters targeting; a tap or long-press paints; STRIKE again or Esc cancels; C4 mode cancels targeting. The spec's "enemy sees the dot or the beam within 4 tiles of it" now samples the dot and the last 4 beam tiles (it only checked the dot). During the inbound countdown the lethal ring is a pulsing red fill (the 1-px dotted ring vanished on jungle), and if WREN is inside it the screen says so and OVERWATCH calls "danger close".
- **Map-wide alarms count once**: a strike (or the Spire falling) raises every alert group at once; the debrief used to show one alarm per group (9 for one strike). Alarms raised together for the same reason count as one.
- **Searchlights** are instant detection for the tower that owns the beam (SPEC §8.1), with LOS from the lamp; shooting the lens ends it. Tower gunners and turrets also no longer see WREN while he is hidden (in a culvert, aboard the dropship), and a strike flash on them wears off after 5 s like on infantry (it used to last forever — only infantry counted `blindT` down).
- **Darkness** is drawn over the sprites and under muzzle flashes/explosions, so fire reads at night. Mission 4 is "day, fading to dusk": an optional `lightFade` (seconds) ramps the dusk layer in; M4 uses 600 s. (Perception already treats M4 as dusk from the start — unchanged from M8.)
- **Culvert (M6)**: the town's west wall had a one-tile gap exactly where the culvert runs, which made the tunnel pointless; the wall is now solid (validator still finds two routes to the cells via the gates). The spec's "one Sniffer inside" is the tunnel's `guard`: it lives hidden in the dark, and going through while it lives adds 2 s and costs one bite (18 HP) — WREN kills it underground (counts as a kill; no body, no witnesses). Tapping a discovered mouth walks there and goes through.
- **Checkpoints**: M6 saves after the rescue, M7 once the shield is down (memory only, SPEC §17.8). A failed debrief offers QUIT / RESTART / CHECKPOINT; the pause menu gets LAST CHECKPOINT. A fresh start drops the checkpoint. Snapshots also carry strike craters, fallout, the shift-change clock and M7's recon intel.
- **Mission clock** (M6 shift change) sits under the minimap buttons, red and blinking for the last 10 s.
- **M7 recon intel** marks the jammers, shield, Spire and power plant on the minimap (blinking dots until actually seen); every known, powered jammer shows its 14-tile coverage as violet hatching on the minimap. The "stealth through the breach" secondary fails on any Alarm while the breach objective is active.
- The scope view stays daylight-bright at night (the lens is the one place you can read targets); darkness is a world-view layer only.

## Iteration 6 — playtest feedback

- **Mission 1 eased (wider gaps)**. Measured with the new `tools/stealth-report.js` (runs the real AI headless with WREN hidden, records per tile how often a crouched soldier there is in someone's sight, then finds the least-watched route to each objective): before, every quiet route crossed tiles watched 76–100 % of the time and the ford was worse than the north bridge even after the tutorial shot; a scripted player who took the ford shot was found and killed within ~10 s. Changes: the east-bank walkers stay out of rifle earshot (12 tiles) of the west-bank spit, so the first scope shot doesn't bring a search party across; the road walker guards the obvious track to the west ramp (south half of the road) instead of the whole road; the hilltop loop keeps to the south half (quiet goat-trail top); the spore loop keeps to the field's north edge (tall grass to the south is the observer's seat); the motor-pool Lobber faces into the compound; the spore sentry faces north/east; the hill-foot walker covers only the south slope; the Skitter waits longer at the ends of its road; two redundant units removed (a doubled hilltop sentry and the road walker's partner). After: with the ford sentry down, the quietest route to each OBSERVE point has no tile watched more than 14 % of the time, and the scripted player (walking, not crawling) observes all three undetected. The obvious approaches (north bridge, west ramp, the road) stay guarded; the validator still finds two routes to each objective.
- **Buildings are no longer hidden by their own footprint (M2 Fuel Depot from the West Ridge)**. A building's rifle target is a point in its footprint (centre column, bottom row). Seen side-on across the canyon, the line to that point crossed the building's own near column (Fuel Depot `blockH` 0.5 on the level-2 mesa), so LOS failed from every tile of the ridge and tapping or long-pressing the depot said "NO FIRING POSITION" — crouched or hunkered. Now (`src/combat/targeting.js`, used by the engage planner and the scope) a building is in sight when any footprint tile is, it counts as visible when any footprint tile is out of the fog, and range is measured to the nearest edge of the footprint. From the ridge rim the depot's near edge is 8–10 tiles away: a hunkered shot.
- **Long shots hunker for range** (deviation from SPEC §5.3, which only plans crouched firing tiles): the engage planner also considers tiles that are in range only when hunkered (the extra 2 tiles), at +4 score, and on arrival WREN hunkers (1 s, "LONG SHOT — HUNKERING FOR RANGE") before the scope opens. Without it, a target reachable only by a hunkered shot either failed or sent WREN on a long walk to a crouch-range tile (for the depot: into the base). Applies to enemies too; walking up to ~4 tiles closer is still preferred over hunkering.
- **Noise indicator** (`src/ui/noise.js`). Noise never fills the detection meter; every enemy within a noise's radius (straight line, walls don't muffle) turns suspicious and walks over to look, and gunshots put the base on Caution — so the thing to show is *reach*. Every noise the NOT react to is drawn as a dotted ring expanding to its true radius over the fog (footsteps pale, wading splash-white, shots amber with echoes, explosions red); the enemy's own gunfire and the map-wide strike are not drawn. The status panel gains a NOISE row: speaker icon, 5-bar meter, SILENT / QUIET / LOUD / VERY LOUD and "CARRIES n TILES" — live footstep noise while moving (running 2.5, shallow water 3, swamp 2, snow/gravel 1), else the last sound's reach held for 1.5 s. M1's noise tip points at the ring and the meter.
- **Tall grass & shallow water cover** (`src/render/terrainCover.js`). Anyone standing in tall grass is drawn with a clump of blades in the biome's own grass colours covering them to about the hips (tapered, gappy, swaying; parts when moving); anyone in shallow water is cut at the waist with a foam line and a ripple ring. WREN, enemies and freed friendlies alike; bodies stay fully visible. The forest x-ray silhouette is unchanged. WREN leaves a trail a few tiles long (16 points, 0.35 tiles apart): a trodden streak with bent blades in tall grass that springs back over 6 s, or spreading wake rings in water (1.8 s). The trail is visual only (the NOT don't follow it — unlike M3's snow tracks). Gameplay: standing still or crouched in shallow water multiplies the detection fill by 0.75 (`detection.waterStill`; only head and shoulders show) — wading itself stays noisy (3 tiles).
- **Low crawl** (deviation from SPEC §6.2, where hunker has speed 0 and a ground tap gets up first). Flat on the ground, a tap now low-crawls there: 0.45 tiles/s, still the hunker stance (so vehicles and turrets still can't see WREN), visibility 0.35 while moving (0.25 still), silent on dry ground and half the terrain noise in shallow water/swamp; a crawl ends flat. Double-tap (run) or the HUNKER button gets up (0.7 s; HUNKER stops the crawl where it is). Only the player's ground tap crawls: automated moves (engage planner, C4, freeing captives, culvert) still get up and walk. Followers keep their heads down and creep at half speed while WREN crawls. The crawl uses the prone sprite for now (proper crawl animation: step 8).
- **Silent takedown** (`src/combat/takedown.js`). An infantry soldier within 1.4 tiles on the same level who isn't in combat and hasn't got WREN at ≥ 0.5 on his meter can be killed without a shot: TAKEDOWN button (appears above SMOKE only when someone is in reach), tapping them, or E. No ammunition; a 1.5-tile scuffle noise; the body stays where it falls; anyone who *sees* it happen is a witness as usual (the knife's witness radius is the scuffle, not the explosion radius). WREN stays in his stance (a takedown from a crawl leaves him flat). Vehicles, gunners in towers/nests and untouchable targets (Vrask on his bunker run) can't be taken down. One-time tips for the crawl and the first takedown in reach. M1's noise tip now comes with the first rifle shot rather than the ford sentry's death (which may now be a silent takedown).
- **Art style switch — the current sprites are "Classic"** (`src/render/artStyle.js`). Before any redesign, every sprite lookup (map infantry `unitSprite`, `vehicleSprite`, scope close-ups `scopeSprite` / `vehicleScopeSprite`) first asks the art registry: redesigned sprites register per kind and type, and are drawn only while ART STYLE is NEW — sprite by sprite, falling back to Classic wherever no redesign exists yet. Cache keys carry the style, so switching is instant, even mid-mission. Settings → ART STYLE (CLASSIC default, saved; shows "NEW (NONE YET)" until redesigns land); `?art=classic|new` compares without touching the saved setting. Code-level fallback: commit `b0fac56` (main after Step 5) is the last commit before any redesign — the environment could not push a git tag (HTTP 403 on tag refs), so the commit is recorded here instead.
- **New art style — infantry** (`src/render/model3d.js`, `src/render/spriteData/newInfantry.js`). New-style foot soldiers are posed 3D figures (capsules, ellipsoids, boxes, cylinders) ray-cast into pixel art from the game's 3/4 camera (34° elevation, light from the top-left), 4×4 supersampled with thin parts (barrels, eyes, antennas) weighted so they survive, a dark exterior outline and shadow lines where one body part overlaps another. All 8 directions are rendered (no mirroring, so the rifle stays in the right hand and the light stays top-left) for idle, walk ×4, run ×4, crouch, cover, fire ×2, pistol ×4, prone ×4, death ×4 (the body falls backwards and stays as the corpse). Figures are 1.3 px per model unit: WREN ≈ 1 tile tall, the lanky NOT ≈ 1.3 tiles (Classic ≈ 0.9) — the extra height is what makes the scope close-up readable. NOT stay charcoal with violet (pauldrons, spine, hocks) and lime (eyes, chest core, belt, knees, hands) per SPEC §12.1; reverse knees and three-segment arms are modelled, not painted. Pilot and scientist get the same body in their own colours.
- **Scope = the map sprite, magnified**: with New art the scope no longer swaps in a hand-drawn close-up; it draws the unit's own map frame (same pose, facing and animation frame) ×4, and every pixel carries the hit zone of the body part it shows (`zoneMap`, resolved by `zoneAtMap`; assisted aim / difficulty grow the test radius instead of boxes). Consequence: a weak spot is exposed by which way the unit faces *on screen* (Scorcher tank, Warden radio, Launcher pod: facing away or sideways; Lobber belt: facing you or sideways), not by the angle from WREN as in Classic. Model placement keeps SPEC §12.1's rules (tests check tank/pod/radio are hidden from the front); from behind, the edge of the Lobber's belt pods can show at the hips — what you see is what you hit. Shots pass *through* an arm held across the body into what is right behind it (so centre mass is still the chest), weapons never take hits, and the Warden's whip antenna is not a target. Vrask's helmet is its own zone and disappears (sprite variant `nohelm`) once shot off. Scope labels drop the FRONT/SIDE word for magnified sprites.
- **WREN hunkered and crawling (New art)**. Hunkered, WREN is a sniper flat behind the rifle on its bipod, legs spread and toes dug in, under a ghillie scrim over back and helmet (a `mottle` material: blotchy olive-to-dry-grass shading fixed to the surface), with the visor still catching the light. Crawling is a separate sprite pose, `crawl` (the Operative reports it while `mode === 'crawl'`): a 4-frame forearm crawl — one arm reaches while the opposite knee draws up, the hips swing to one side between reaches, the rifle rides along the right forearm. Classic draws `crawl` with its one flat pose, so Classic is unchanged.
- **New art style — vehicles** (`src/render/spriteData/newVehicles.js`, shared posing kit `src/render/rig3d.js`). Skitter, Hauler, Fuel Hauler, Crawler, Brute, Juggernaut and the GOD medical truck are built from the same primitives and ray-cast at the same scale as the infantry, in 8 facings plus `wreck` (the same hull charred, settled and askew, turret knocked round) and `nodriver` (`vehicleState`: once the driver is shot, the seat is empty). The scope magnifies the map sprite exactly as for infantry, with per-pixel zones `driver`, `jerrycan`, `tank`, `slit`, `turret`, `hull`. The **Skitter** is an open buggy whose driver sits high and large — the big weak spot from every side (the classic close-ups hid him from behind; an open buggy seen from above cannot); its jerrycans ride on the rear deck. **Hauler / Fuel Hauler** cabs are open-topped behind a windscreen frame with a tall back wall, so the driver is a target from the front and both sides but not from behind (the SPEC §12.2 rule — a closed cab roof hid him from the sides at this camera angle). The **fuel tank** is orange from the sides, rear and top — seen from above, its top also shows over the cab from the front, and what you see is what you hit. **Armour** keeps a tiny glowing view slit set flush into the front plate: visible head-on and from the front diagonals only (tests check none from the sides or back); hitting turret or hull still only rings off the armour.
- **Cost and warm-up**: a frame renders in ~2 ms (precomputed ray constants, per-pixel primitive lists). On mission start the game queues every frame its infantry will use (common poses first) and draws them in the background within 3 ms per frame (~800 frames in ~3 s on M2, 60 fps held); anything not yet warmed renders on first use. Vehicles are queued too (~4 ms each; M3 warms ~1 050 frames in ~3.5 s at 60 fps). Overhead markers (?/!, radio plate, takedown bracket, WREN's chevron and busy bar, escort HP pips) rise by however much taller the New figure is than Classic's (`markerLift`); vehicle icons sit just above the roof.
- **New art is the default** (save v2): `DEFAULT_SETTINGS.artStyle = 'new'`; saves from before v2 stored `classic` only because it was the default then, so they move to New once — a Classic choice made afterwards sticks. The `Art` module itself still starts Classic (headless tests use the classic hit boxes unless they switch).
- **Story & cutscenes** (`src/missions/story.js` script, `src/scenes/cutscene.js` player). A 90s action-movie arc over the seven missions (the approved outline; the M4 pilot is now Lt. Idris Vale — "Kane" is a banned name). Every mission intro is three beats: a nature close-up (grass, a leaf with dew, an insect) → something arrives in it, either the NOT (M2 a Skitter runs the beetle over, M4 a patrol tramples through, M6 a Sniffer noses in, M7 a Juggernaut rolls over everything) or WREN (M1 low-crawls in, M3 a pine needle and M5 a leaf settle on the rifle scope, then a glove brushes it off) → an establishing shot with WREN in the foreground and the mission beyond (Cherry Hill across the Varna, the Anvil Mesa plant, the Crawler convoy crawling up the pass, the patrol on the canyon floor below, the Delta Island refinery, Saint Ives' searchlights in the rain, the Hive Spire under its shield), the title card and the radio lines. Plus the start intro (spore pods land, the GOD map table, WREN "retired" at the tree line, title slam), a short "SIGNAL LOST" sting on failure, and the ending (the Spire topples, the NOT scatter, the dropship lifts WREN out, credits with "Inspired by the real-time strategy games of the 1990s."). Drawn procedurally into a half-resolution buffer (reads as pixel art); soldiers and vehicles are the New 3D models rendered big (`zoom` on `renderInfantry`/`renderVehicle`, 2×2 supersampling). Rules: tap / Esc / Enter / Space skips; the intro plays on every START and each mission intro every time the mission is picked from the map (playtest: first-time-only made them seem gone), and both can be replayed (title INTRO, briefing SCENE); retries, LOAD SAVE and CONTINUE-into-a-save go straight back into the action; the failure sting plays on every failure, the ending on every M7 win; reduced motion drops shake, flashes, flicker and drift; `?nocut=1` / `?debug=1` skip them all, `?cut=<id>` plays one directly (intro, m1–m7, failed, ending).
- **Saving** (deviation from SPEC §17.8, where checkpoints lived in memory for one attempt). Mid-mission saves go into the save file (browser localStorage, key `westwood-sniper:v1`, one slot per mission, `save.resume`): **autosave** a moment after every completed objective ("PROGRESS SAVED"), the scripted M6/M7 checkpoints, and **QUICK SAVE** (HUD disk button next to pause, pause menu, F5 — the browser reload is suppressed). Quick save is refused while an enemy has WREN detected, while scoped, busy or in a tunnel, so a save can't lock in a death. **LOAD SAVE** (pause menu, the failure debrief, F9) reloads the mission and restores it; a fresh RESTART keeps the save loadable; winning the mission clears its slot. CONTINUE on the title screen resumes the most recent mid-mission save (surviving a closed browser), else opens the campaign map. Fog in snapshots is run-length encoded (a save is ~12 KB). Campaign progress (unlocked missions, stars, medals) was already saved on every debrief.
- **Sound & music** (`src/audio/sfx.js`, SPEC §16): everything is synthesised with Web Audio — no sample files. The context starts on the first user gesture (autoplay rules); headless, every call is a silent no-op. Effects for the rifle (crack, thump, echo), pistol, NOT energy rifles, bolt/reload/dry fire, scope in/out, headshot ping, explosions and the strike, rockets, throws, C4 beeps, the strike whistle and designator hum, pickups, breath, splashes, radio squelch, objective chime, save blip, alarm siren, the "spotted" stinger and the takedown scuffle. Music: a 90s synth theme (A minor, 104 bpm: pad, driving bass, drums, a square-wave lead) on the title, map, briefing and cutscenes; an adaptive mission track (88 bpm) that is a pad and a lone distant ping while WREN is hidden, adds bass and arpeggio when an enemy is suspicious or an alarm is up, and drums when WREN is detected; win/fail stings on the debrief. Settings' SFX and MUSIC sliders drive the two buses.
- **Tips are modal and point at their subject** (playtest). An OVERWATCH tip now pauses the game until tapped (a tap anywhere closes it; queued tips follow), dims the screen except for a spotlight on what it is about, and draws its box next to that thing with a pointer: WREN for movement/noise/crawl, the nearest tall grass for the grass tip, the nearest visible soldier for cones/first shot, the knoll, the body that was found, the building for C4, Vrask, the lead truck, or a HUD button (R&GUN, STRIKE). If the subject is off-screen the camera goes there and comes back afterwards. New tip: **Run & Gun**, when WREN is detected and two or more soldiers in combat are within 9 tiles.
- **Taps and long-presses** (playtest): a tap on any enemy — soldier, vehicle (anywhere on its body; hit areas scale with zoom) or building with weak points — brings up the scope (an unaware soldier within reach is still a silent takedown); **long-press plants C4** on a building, a disabled vehicle or a bridge (the C4 button + tap still works). The old "tap to propose, tap again to plant" flow is gone.
- **ZOOM button** next to QUICK SAVE toggles 1×/2× (pinch works on some devices only).
- **Scope on touch**: drag to aim, **lift the finger to fire**; release on ✕ or touch a second finger (pinch) to lower the rifle. BREATH stays a button for the other thumb; with a mouse a click still fires.
- **HUD layout** (playtest). System buttons — ZOOM, QUICK SAVE, PAUSE, CENTRE — are one row along the bottom edge, between the status panel and the action grid. The action buttons form a fixed 2×3 grid under the minimap: R&GUN · COVER / HUNKER · SMOKE / TAKEDOWN · C4 (BOOM once charges are down). Buttons no longer appear and disappear: TAKEDOWN, C4 and SMOKE are always there and greyed out when they can't be used; tapping a greyed one explains it once (a modal tip pointing at the button: how takedowns work, long-press to plant C4, what smoke does), then just says why ("NO ONE IN REACH", "NO C4 LEFT", "NO SMOKE LEFT"). Anything a mission adds opens a new row under the grid: STRIKE (strategic strike, M5–M7) bottom right, with its own tip the first time it is available; ADVANCE/HOLD (convoy) and FOLLOW/HOLD (escorts) on the left. Grid buttons shrink to fit under the minimap on short screens; a label too wide for its button uses a short form (TAKEDOWN → T-DOWN). Status panel: the ammo reads `RIFLE 5/5 | 15`, and the stance moved to the bottom line next to the noise meter ("CARRIES n TILES" moved up a line).

## Hosting

- **Cloudflare Workers (static assets)**: `wrangler.jsonc` in the repo; `npx wrangler deploy` runs `node tools/build-site.js`, which copies only `index.html`, `icon.svg`, `manifest.webmanifest` and `src/` into `dist/` (git-ignored), and only `dist/` is uploaded. Without a committed config, Wrangler's auto-setup served the repo root and tried to upload `node_modules/` (its own 128 MiB `workerd` binary → "Asset too large"); an allow-list also keeps tests, tools and docs off the site, and a separate folder stops `wrangler dev` from reload-looping on its own `.wrangler/` state. Still no bundling and no runtime dependencies; `npm run serve` keeps serving the source tree directly.
